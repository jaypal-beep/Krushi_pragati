Information About the Directorate of Agriculture, Government of Gujarat

The Directorate of Agriculture, Government of Gujarat, plays a crucial role in improving the livelihoods of farmers and promoting sustainable agricultural practices across the state. Through a combination of policy guidance, research, advisory services, and welfare programmes, it enables Gujarat's farmers to adopt modern techniques and grow with confidence.

The Directorate of Agriculture serves as a key organization in the formulation and implementation of Gujarat's agricultural policies. It supports farmers with timely information, inputs, and infrastructure — bridging the latest research with the realities of the field, and driving long-term productivity, profitability, and resilience.

Collaboration and Capacity Building

The Directorate works in coordination with research institutions, agricultural universities, and other government departments to develop sustainable solutions for farmers. Continuous knowledge exchange and capacity-building programs empower farmers, extension staff, and young innovators to adopt modern, climate-resilient practices.

In addition to developing policies and offering support schemes, the Directorate ensures the availability of modern machinery, crop protection measures, and high-quality inputs — driving collective, long-term growth across Gujarat's agricultural sector.
