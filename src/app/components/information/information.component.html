Key Objectives

✦

Accurate Crop Area Estimation

The total crop area cultivated in the fields is measured using remote sensing technology. This information is crucial for agricultural development, state government assistance programs, agricultural policy formulation, and resource allocation.

Key Insights

  • ✦ If a single crop is being cultivated in excess, steps will be taken to promote crop diversification.
  • ✦ Special planning will be undertaken to ensure the optimal use of arable land.
  • ✦ Monitoring changes in farming practices and sowing patterns will help develop strategies for irrigation and fertilizer management.

Planting Pattern Monitoring

Identify shifts in planting patterns, enabling timely adjustments in resource allocation and agronomic practices.

Crop Production and Management

This report is data-driven, making it essential for strategic planning, enabling better crop production management and quick response during crises.

Methods and Technology

Crop area and sowing analysis are conducted using the following advanced technologies:

  • ✦ High-Resolution Satellite Imagery – Observing and monitoring crop coverage.
  • ✦ Remote Sensing and GIS Technology – Analyzing sowing patterns and cropping trends.
  • ✦ Surveys – Collecting ground-level data with the assistance of farmers and officials to verify and validate reports.

Usage in Water Management and Irrigation Planning

Irrigation Management: This information ensures proper assessment of water requirements and timely availability of water.

  • ✦ Optimal Land Utilization: Enables effective pest control and fertilizer management tailored to soil and crop needs.
  • ✦ Pest Control and Crop Protection: Adopts a scientific, data-driven approach to managing crop diseases and invasive pests.
  • ✦ Surveys – Collecting ground-level data with the assistance of farmers and officials to verify and validate reports.

State-Level Planning and Agricultural Development Utilization

This report serves as a strong tool for agricultural development at the state level.

The state government can formulate better policies for agricultural support and market management.

  • ✦ Resource Management: Effective planning for land, water, fertilizers, and seeds.
  • ✦ Risk Management: Identifying agricultural risks and developing immediate solutions.
